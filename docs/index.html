Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I sign in?
    Tap "Sign in with GitHub" and authorize the app with your GitHub account. Your credentials are stored securely on your device.
  • Why is my quota not loading?
    Ensure you have an active internet connection and a valid GitHub subscription that includes usage quotas. Pull down to refresh.
  • How do I sign out?
    Go to Settings and tap "Sign Out". This removes all stored data from your device.
  • Is my data shared with anyone?
    No. All data stays on your device. We do not collect analytics, track users, or send data to any server. See our Privacy Policy.
  • How do I add the home screen widget?
    On iOS, long-press your home screen, tap "+", and search for "CodeMeter". On Android, long-press and select Widgets.
